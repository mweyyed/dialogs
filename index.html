Your host, Badia Berrag

مقدّمة البرنامج

Badia studies English Language Teaching at Marmara University in Istanbul as a Türkiye Maarif Foundation scholar. She works as a research intern at Enstitü Sosyal, focusing on knowledge production around Muslim societies, and as a freelance translator. She has also written as a journalism intern at Fokus+, taught English at Istanbul University’s language club, and been named Best Delegate by the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation.

Her writing and research return to the same questions this podcast asks: identity, language, education, and how ideas travel across the Maghreb and beyond.
